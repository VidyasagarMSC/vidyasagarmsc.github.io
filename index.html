About

A builder who learned to
shape the system, not just the code.

I started by writing code. Over 19+ years that turned into shaping the systems, teams, and strategies that make large-scale technology work — from telecom software at Mahindra Satyam to enterprise strategy for IBM Cloud.

Today I sit at the intersection of architecture and operations. I lead the design and onboarding of complex cloud and AI workloads for IBM's Platinum-level clients in finance and healthcare, while managing globally distributed engineering teams across IBM Cloud's MISP portfolio and AIOps capabilities.

The work demands fluency across the whole stack — SAP ASE, VSI on VPC, z Systems, VMware, ROKS/IKS, and modern DevSecOps pipelines — and the judgement to know which one a problem actually needs.

An IBM Level 3 Thought Leader and elected BCS Fellow, I'm the first person from India to hold the Microsoft MVP award for Games (2014–2017). I write, speak, and curate open resources because the best technical ideas only compound when they're shared.
