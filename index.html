About

Still a coder.
Now also the manager, the architect, the researcher.

My first real job was writing .NET for FIFA-EMS — the platform behind the 2010 World Cup — from an office in Hyderabad. Five companies and one 19-year detour later (Tech Mahindra, Aptean, Indecomm, Dell R&D), I joined IBM in 2015, and in 2022 its US-based Global Cloud Acceleration team brought me to Austin, where I've been ever since.

The roles tell the story better than any title. I've been a developer evangelist getting Asia-Pacific developers onto IBM Cloud, a product manager writing the solution tutorials people still follow, a solutions architect building landing zones for financial ISVs — and now both a manager, running a globally distributed engineering organisation for IBM Cloud's MISP portfolio and AIOps, and a principal architect, onboarding the hardest finance and healthcare workloads onto the platform. I never did pick a side of the fence; I keep one foot in the code.

Off-hours, I'm a researcher at heart: quantum computing — Quantum Ideas Summer School, IBM's quantum-safe cryptography certification — agentic AI, and the mathematics of optimisation, which is where my 2026 focus sits. I write 200+ articles on all of it for over a million readers, judge watsonx challenge use cases, and run BlueCoders, the meetup community I founded. The honours — BCS Fellow, Open Group Distinguished Architect, the first Microsoft MVP for Games from India — are lovely, but the best part is still shipping something that works.
